Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026. This policy covers the independent watch-only Super Watch app, operated by Tavoun Dixon, and this support website. It does not describe the separate, unreleased iPhone prototype.

Health access is optional

For workouts you start, Super Watch requests read access to heart rate and active energy, and permission to save workouts and active energy to Apple Health. Sleep import separately requests read access to existing sleep records. Data is used to show workout measurements and sleep history on your device. The app does not independently detect overnight sleep, infer sleep stages or diagnose conditions.

You may decline access. Art displays, manual self-rated stress check-ins and recovery pauses do not require Health access. A recorded workout requires permission to save workouts. Missing read permission or missing records may appear as no readable data; Apple does not reveal whether you denied read access.

Data stays on your device

The app does not transmit health records or preferences to the developer. It has no developer-operated backend, analytics SDK, advertising, tracking or account system. Your selected display and stress rating are stored in local app preferences. Imported sleep and live workout values are processed in app memory. Saved workouts are stored in Apple Health, which may sync or back up according to your Apple settings. We do not sell health data or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Your display and stress preferences remain until changed or the app is removed. Use Clear rating to clear a stress rating. Manage Health access and delete saved workouts through Apple Health and your device settings. Removing Super Watch does not itself delete records previously saved in Apple Health. Apple's sync, backup and retention settings apply separately. We cannot retrieve or delete Health records on your behalf.

Support email

If you voluntarily email relaycivic@gmail.com, the recipient and email service process your email address and message to respond. Do not send health records, passwords or identifying screenshots. Support correspondence may remain in the support mailbox; you can request its deletion by email, subject to any applicable retention obligation. No support message is sent automatically by the app.

This website

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, tracking cookies, forms or third-party embedded media. GitHub may process ordinary request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure hosting. See GitHub's privacy statement. Visiting this website does not send your Apple Health records to it.

Updates and questions

Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or support-email deletion requests, contact relaycivic@gmail.com.
