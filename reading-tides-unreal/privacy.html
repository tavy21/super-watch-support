Reading Tides Privacy

Reading Tides saves lesson progress and discoveries on your device. The game has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, purchases or developer backend. Your answers and progress are not sent to us. No camera, microphone, contacts or location access is needed.

Deleting the app through iPhone Settings removes its local data. A device backup may restore saved progress. The game does not provide cloud synchronization.

If you contact relaycivic@gmail.com for help, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Please do not include children’s names or other sensitive information. A parent or guardian should handle support correspondence.

Supplemental practice, not a validated curriculum or assessment. Play while stationary, away from real water and traffic.
