Reading Tides Support

Tap Start voyage, then tap an answer card or swipe left or right to steer the boat. Sail through the matching answer. Rocks and driftwood slow you briefly; there are no lives or paid retries.

Gentle pace provides more time. Chapter buttons explore seven groups of reading practice. Wrong answers repeat with guidance; correct answers earn discoveries. Progress saves locally. Pause holds your place; leaving the app pauses the voyage.

The Unreal edition contains 42 written practice prompts and does not currently narrate them. Read together with a grown-up. Parents, privacy & help is available from the start and pause panels.

For help, a grown-up can email relaycivic@gmail.com with the app version, iPhone model and a description of the issue. Do not include children’s names or sensitive information. Reopen the app if necessary; avoid reinstalling unless you are prepared to lose local progress.

No subscription, paid API or internet connection is required to play.

Supplemental practice, not a validated curriculum or assessment. Play while stationary, away from real water and traffic.
