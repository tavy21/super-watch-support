Reading Tides Support

Choose a chapter, then Set sail. Steer with arrows or swipes. Dodge obstacles before choosing a reading answer. Wrong answers repeat with a hint; collisions slow you down without removing discoveries. Pause stops the course; Chapters returns to the chooser. Progress saves after a correct answer.

If audio is unavailable, read together; an English voice must already be available on the device. If scenery fails to load, reopen the app. Do not reinstall unless you are willing to lose local progress.

Free offline reading practice. No subscription. Supplemental practice, not a validated curriculum or assessment. Play while stationary, away from real water and traffic.
