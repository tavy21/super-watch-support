Reading Tides Privacy

Reading Tides does not collect data. Progress stays on your device. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, purchases or developer backend. Optional spoken words use a local device voice; no microphone or audio recording is used. No data is shared with the developer. Deleting the app removes local progress.

Free offline reading practice. No subscription. Supplemental practice, not a validated curriculum or assessment. Play while stationary, away from real water and traffic.
