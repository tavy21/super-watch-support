One meal. Better timing.

MealCue coordinates the dishes you cook together on iPhone and Apple Watch, with local iPad planning too. No account, subscription, ads or developer-hosted service.

Start a meal

  1. Choose an example or create a meal. Enter your own dish names and estimated durations.
  2. Tap Cook this meal. MealCue plans backwards from the longest dish.
  3. Tap Start when each dish actually begins cooking. Check food and tap Mark done when appropriate.

Running late?

+5 and +10 minutes move the serving target and pending starts. They never extend timers for food already cooking. Starting a step later than planned moves the remaining schedule. Check safe holding conditions for food that finishes earlier, or serve sooner. MealCue does not measure doneness or food safety.

Sync your phone and Watch

Install MealCue on your paired iPhone and Watch and open both apps. Saved meals sync in both directions without a cloud account. Use Phone & Watch sync to see status and retry. Updates may wait until the devices reconnect. Editing different meals offline preserves both; editing the same meal on both devices can result in one version winning, so edit one device at a time.

A cooking session remains controlled on the device where it started. Open Cooking on iPhone or Cooking on Apple Watch on the companion to view the latest received state. That view may be stale while disconnected. Start, finish and delay controls and local alerts remain on the controlling device. Independent sessions can run on both devices. iPad cannot pair with a Watch.

Reminders and saved plans

Enable cooking alerts when prompted, and check system notification settings if you do not receive them. Timers use saved timestamps and resume after reopening. Notification delivery depends on system settings; always supervise cooking. Up to 20 meals can be created per library, each with up to 8 dishes and durations of 1–240 minutes.
