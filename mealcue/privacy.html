MealCue privacy policy · Effective November 21, 2026

On-device cooking plans

MealCue is operated by Tavoun Dixon. Saved meal names, dish names, durations and cooking sessions are stored on your devices. The app has no account, developer backend, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK or paid API. MealCue does not send your cooking plans to the developer.

iPhone and Apple Watch

MealCue automatically exchanges saved meals and the latest cooking-session state between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. A locally generated installation identifier and logical revision numbers resolve updates; these are used only between the companion apps. Watch pairing is unavailable on iPad. Sync may wait until the paired devices reconnect or wake.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, MealCue schedules local reminders on the device controlling your cooking session. MealCue does not use a remote notification server. You can change notification permission in system Settings.

Storage, backup and deletion

Delete a saved meal from its Meal plan screen. The deletion syncs to your paired device when connectivity is available; a small deletion record is retained to prevent an older offline copy from reappearing. End a cooking session to remove its active plan. Removing the app deletes its local app container; remove it from both devices to remove both copies. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your settings and are managed separately through Apple.

Support and this website

If you email relaycivic@gmail.com, your email address and message are handled by our support mailbox and email provider. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by email.

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, with no added analytics, cookies or embedded media. GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses for hosting and security. GitHub privacy statement.

Policy updates appear here with a revised effective date. Contact relaycivic@gmail.com with privacy questions.
