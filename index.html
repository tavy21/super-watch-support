An independent Apple Watch app

A little art.
A moment to move.

Explore 24 in-app artistic clock displays, from galaxies and water to nostalgic objects and historic art. Start workouts manually, explore existing Apple Health sleep history, record how stressed you feel, and take a short recovery pause.

Super Watch is preparing for its first App Store release. No download is available here yet.

Private by design

No account, ads, analytics or developer-operated health-data service. Health access is optional. Artistic displays run inside the app and do not replace Apple's system watch faces.
