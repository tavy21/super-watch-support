Help on your wrist

Super Watch support

Email relaycivic@gmail.com. Include your app version, watch model, watchOS version and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not include health records, passwords or private account details. There is no guaranteed response time.

Choose an artistic display

Open Choose a face, select a design, then open My display. Your selection is saved locally. The 24 designs work inside Super Watch; they cannot replace Apple's system watch faces.

Workouts and permissions

Open Workout and choose a supported activity to request Health access and start a recorded session. You can pause, resume and end the session. Repetitions and sets are entered manually. Heart rate and active energy appear only when Apple Health supplies them. Granting access does not guarantee a sensor reading.

If a workout does not start, check Health permissions in Apple Health or device settings. If saving fails, read the in-app status before starting another session. The app does not automatically detect and start activities.

Empty sleep history

Open Sleep history and choose Import Apple Health. Allow sleep read access and ensure Apple Health already contains sleep records. The app imports existing records; it does not independently monitor overnight sleep. No readable records may mean no data or limited permission. Overlapping asleep intervals are counted once.

Stress and recovery

Stress check-in is your own rating, not a measured physiological or medical score. Clear rating removes it. Recovery pause provides a short timer; it does not diagnose or treat a condition. Super Watch is a general wellness app, not a medical device.

Privacy and removal

No login or subscription is required by this build. Review the privacy policy for Health permissions, local storage and deletion. Removing the app does not delete workouts already saved in Apple Health.

Artwork

Includes original app designs and public-domain Hokusai and Van Gogh artwork from The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access collection. In-app artwork credits identify the included works.
