WristReel support

Start on iPhone

  1. Choose Photos or Files and select a video you have permission to use.
  2. Use a clip up to two minutes long. WristReel prepares it locally.
  3. Tap Add instruction card to add step text and an optional timer.
  4. Tap Send to Watch. Delivery is scheduled by Apple and may take time; keep the devices paired with connectivity enabled.
  5. Open WristReel on Watch, select the clip and play or replay it offline.

Limits and troubleshooting

Prepared clips must fit within 40 MB, with a 250 MB library per device. Protected videos are not supported. Reduce the source clip length if preparation fails. Install the Watch companion app before sending. Cancel pending transfers and send again after editing a card. Deleting a clip on one device does not delete it on another.

Timers are for steps while the card remains open. They are not background alarms. Physical-device behavior may vary with watchOS, connectivity and battery conditions.

YouTube

WristReel saves links for opening on iPhone. It does not play or download YouTube videos on Watch.

Cost

No subscriptions, in-app purchases, paid APIs or developer cloud service. Device storage and any existing internet or cloud-provider charges remain your own.
