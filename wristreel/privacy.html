Privacy, on your terms.

WristReel privacy policy · Effective November 21, 2026

Your videos stay with you

WristReel is operated by Tavoun Dixon. Videos, instruction text, timers and saved YouTube links are processed and stored on your devices. The app has no account system, developer backend, analytics, advertising or tracking SDK. It does not send your library to the developer.

Photos, Files and your Watch

You select individual videos with Apple's Photos or Files picker. WristReel prepares a local copy and, when you tap Send to Watch, sends the clip and its instruction card to your paired Apple Watch using WatchConnectivity. Cloud-based source files may first download through your chosen provider. The app does not request access to your entire photo library.

Storage and deletion

Remove videos separately on each device. Removing a WristReel copy does not delete the original in Photos or Files, or a copy on the other device. Cancel pending transfers separately. Video copies are excluded from system backup; library metadata, instructions and bookmarks may be included according to your Apple backup settings. Removing the app removes its local container; separately retained backups are managed through Apple.

External links and support

YouTube links open on iPhone in an external app or browser. YouTube and your browser then apply their own policies. WristReel does not extract or download YouTube videos. If you email relaycivic@gmail.com, your email address and message are processed by our support mailbox and email provider. Do not send private videos or passwords. You can request deletion of support correspondence by email.

This website

These static pages use GitHub Pages, with no added analytics, cookies or embedded media. GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses for hosting and security. GitHub privacy statement.
