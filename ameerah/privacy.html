Ameerah’s World Privacy Policy

Updated November 21, 2026. Published by Tavoun Dixon.

Offline play, no developer data collection

The iPhone game works using bundled content. It does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases, subscriptions, multiplayer chat or developer-hosted backend. It does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

Progress on your device

Stars, habits, badges, learning progress and sound preferences are stored locally in the app. Device backups may include this data under your Apple settings. Deleting the app removes its local data. There is no developer cloud account or cross-device synchronization.

Children and families

The game asks for no names, dates of birth or contact details. Optional spoken affirmations use the device’s system speech functionality. The game contains no external links. A privacy and help panel is available inside the game.

If you contact support

Email relaycivic@gmail.com. Messages are handled through the email provider and used to respond to your request; they are not collected automatically by gameplay. Please do not send children’s personal information. Request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address, subject to any retention required by law.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, whose service may process basic request information such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy. The game does not load these pages during play. These pages contain no advertising or added analytics.

Changes

We will update this policy and the App Store disclosures if the app’s practices change.
