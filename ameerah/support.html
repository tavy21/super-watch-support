Ameerah’s World Support

A free, offline 3D life-and-learning adventure for iPhone.

Getting started

Tap Wake Up & Play. Use the arrow pad to walk and Jump to jump. Drag the scenery to turn the camera. Tap the gold activity prompt near an activity. Show Controls opens travel, camera, sound and sky controls. The Adventure journal tracks habits, school goals and kindness.

Progress and sound

Progress saves on your device when storage is available. Uninstalling removes local progress. No account or cloud sync is available. Toggle sound with the speaker control. Spoken affirmations depend on system voice availability. For a display issue, close and reopen the app; do not uninstall if you want to keep local progress.

For grown-ups

Read alongside younger players. This game is playful practice, not professional education or a replacement for supervision. The 3D world requires visual and touch interaction; full screen-reader gameplay is not currently supported.

Contact

Email relaycivic@gmail.com with the app version, iPhone model and a description of the issue. Do not include a child’s personal details.

No ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases or paid service requirements. iOS 17 or later.
